Top 10 New Manga Isekai

Manga Isekai lately has been filled with nothing but garbage, but don’t be disappointed because there's some good new manga Isekai out there. Click the image if you're interested in the manga.

10. Mezametara Saikyou Soubi to Uchuusenmochi Datta node, Ikkodate Mezashite Youhei toshite Jiyuu ni Ikitai

Even from the title alone, it sounds like your average trashsekai, but it's like a pure diamond inside. From the world-building to the character development, and most importantly, the MC is not naive.

9. Akuyaku Reijou Level 99: Watashi wa UraBoss desu ga Maou de wa Arimasen

A really good villainous shojo manga, it's cute, wholesome, and makes you smirk out of nowhere. Highly recommended for light-hearted fun.

8. Yajin Tensei: Karate Survivor in Another World

The myth, the legend, the man himself Yajin. A great story with fantastic pacing and an MC who just can’t catch a break.

7. Yasei yasei no last boss ga arawareta!

This manga is about our mc got isekai'ed into his game character. The story is really good, the lore(yes lore im not kidding) is sooo good, the story telling is top notch(sometimes). Really worth to give it a try.

6. Kumo Desu ga, Nani ka?

A spider? so what? is a gold isekai, you got cute, you got action, you got story, you got the fucking useless hero. its perfect everything to the last drop is perfect.

5. Chounin A wa Akuyaku Reijou wo Doushitemo Sukuitai ~Dobu to Sora to Koori no Himegimi~

Mobuseka but short, mobuseka but straight forward, mobuseka but sex(yeah no kidding our mc is nuts with our heroine)and mobuseka but WAR CRIME BABY LETSGOOO.
